# How to Change Your Password in Microsoft 365 Outlook

2026-11-21 · @JT Mauro

Hi All –

Below is a quick guide to changing your Microsoft 365 password in Outlook. It takes about 2 minutes. You will need your current password and your phone for the sign-in approval.

## Steps

1. Open your browser and type outlook.office365.com, then log in to your mail.
2. Click your initials or photo in the top-right corner, then select View account.

    *[Screenshot 1: Outlook profile menu with View account highlighted]*

3. In the My Account section, select Change password.

    *[Screenshot 2: My Account page with Change password highlighted]*

4. You will be asked to sign in again to confirm it is you. Complete the verification.

    *[Screenshot 3: Identity verification prompt]*

5. Enter your new password twice.

    *[Screenshot 4: Change password form with Submit button]*

6. Select Submit. A confirmation message will appear.

## Tips

- Use a long, unique password that meets the company password policy.
- Sign back in on your phone and any other device with the new password. An old password saved on a phone is a common cause of account lockouts.
- If you forgot your password, select Forgot password on the sign-in page, or contact IT at itservicedesk@cydcor.com.
- If your password is rejected or your account is locked, contact IT at itservicedesk@cydcor.com.

If any issues arise, please let us know. We are here to help.
