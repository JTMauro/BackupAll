    PowerPoint: .pptx files open right here, with text, pictures, shapes, tables and embedded videos. For a pixel-perfect copy of complicated slides, save from PowerPoint as PDF (File › Export › PDF) and add the PDF instead. Old .ppt and Keynote files need to be saved as .pptx or PDF first.

    YouTube: works for slides and background music. If a video says it can't play, start VideoBlitz with the Start VideoBlitz launcher in the download folder. Some videos are blocked from playing outside youtube.com by their owners.

    Two screens: press Second screen, drag the new window to the projector or TV and double-click it for full screen. This window becomes your presenter view, with the next slide and speaker notes.
