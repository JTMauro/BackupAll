PLANETGUARD

Landers are lifting colonists off the plateau. Shoot them before they reach the top of the sky. Catch colonists as they fall, or fly low to scoop them off the ground, then drop them at a green shelter pad. You can carry five at once. Your lasers can't hurt colonists: shooting at a colonist being carried hits the lander instead. A colonist carried all the way up turns its lander into a mutant.

Your ship's shield absorbs the first hit. After that, each hit damages the hull: 25% from landers, bombers, swarmers and mines, 50% from mutants, baiters and pods. Once per wave a repair carrier flies in after you take damage. Land on its flight deck to restore the shield and hull.

Every wave comes with a quest for bonus points, and some quests add a smart bomb. Fail the quest and you lose a ship and the wave starts over. Every tenth wave a mothership attacks: knock out its four turrets to drop its shield, then destroy the core. Waves get harder through each set of ten, and each new set starts 5% harder than the last.
