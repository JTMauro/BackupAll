Advisor tips

Opening

Spheres: each power starts with its home country and a few neighbors. Draft: powers take turns claiming one nation at a time until the whole world is taken.

Deploy

Place new troops on your nations. You earn 1 per 3 nations, plus region bonuses and a population bonus.

Combat

Attack bordering nations or ones across a sea lane with Risk dice. Several of your nations can attack one target together. Spend credits on missile strikes from any nation in range.

Fortify

Move troops once per turn through a chain of your own nations.

Diplomacy

Form alliances, non-aggression pacts and trade deals. Partners cannot attack each other. Breaking a treaty ruins your reputation, and allies can share a victory.

Capitals

Capitals cannot be invaded in round 1, and defend with 3 dice and +1 on their best die. Missile shields can intercept incoming strikes. Lose yours and your empire collapses.

Win

Take every rival capital, or control the share of the world you chose (60–100%). A captured capital collapses that power.
