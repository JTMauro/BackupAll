Chip Away FX

builder-fx.js 2.1 + builder-sfx.js + builder-music.js. Everything is procedural: three.js r128, HalfFloat + UnrealBloom (1.05) + gamma; Web Audio.

Guns fire their colour at matching cubes. The drone carries a cube around the sculpture and fires it.
The game's camera: large, from behind, front half visible. Above 1.15 thrust the engines go to afterburner.
makeIsland({ radius, seed, biome }): rock, grass, ice, lava, crystal. The flat centre sits at y = 0. Each island is under 20k triangles.
makeAsteroidBelt({ count, inner, outer, clearInner, clearOuter }): about 2,400 instanced rocks tumbling on the GPU, plus 6 big hero asteroids. Here it's centred on the planet, with the clear ring widened to 120–260 so the gameplay area stays open. The game uses 120–215.
Once sound is on, the gameplay plays its sounds too: lasers, pops, hammer, purge, drone and the planet explosion.
builder-music.js: eight procedural styles with their own volume and mute, separate from the sound effects. Intensity 0.4 for the map, 0.8 in levels.
Guns:
1,000 instanced cubes: roundedBoxGeometry() + makeCubeMaterial() + setColorAt.
Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
