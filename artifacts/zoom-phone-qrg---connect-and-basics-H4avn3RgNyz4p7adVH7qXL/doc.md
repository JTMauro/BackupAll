# Zoom Phone QRG – Connect and Basics

2026-11-17 · @JT Mauro

Your Zoom Phone number works on your cell phone, computer and desk phone, all at once.

## How to connect

- **Cell phone:** Install **Zoom Workplace** (App Store or Google Play) → sign in with your Cydcor account → tap **Phone**. Allow microphone and notifications.
- **Computer:** Install **Zoom Workplace** (zoom.us/download or your software portal) → sign in → click **Phone**. Use a headset.
- **Desk phone:** Plug in network and power → wait for it to boot → lift the handset for a dial tone. Blank after 5 minutes? Contact IT.

## Basics

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Call | Phone → Keypad → number with area code → **Call**. Coworkers: dial the extension. |
| Answer / decline | **Accept** or **Decline** on the alert, or lift the desk phone handset |
| Mute / hold | **Mute** or **Hold** buttons |
| Transfer | **Transfer** → name or number → **Warm** (talk first) or **Blind** (send now) |
| Switch devices | **Flip** moves a live call between cell, computer and desk phone |
| Voicemail | Phone → **Voicemail** |
| 911 | Dial 911 and say your location out loud |
