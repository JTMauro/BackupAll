Burning Lead Assets

Burning Lead asset pack

Deep explosion booms with no high crack, weapon sounds, explosion flipbooks and tileable 1024 px textures with normal maps. Every file below is in the pack under the same name.

Explosions

Weapons

Squad voices (4 male voices, radio or room, shouted)

Explosion flipbooks (8×8 frames, 128 px each, PNG with alpha)

Textures (move the pointer over a swatch to move the light)

Voices are neural text-to-speech (Piper; voices Joe, CC0, and Norman, John and Bryce, public domain), so they're free to use commercially. They were pitched down, compressed and saturated, and half go through a radio band-pass, the rest a short room. Every call-out was checked with speech recognition. The grunts and death sounds are spoken interjections, so they're less raw than a recorded actor.
