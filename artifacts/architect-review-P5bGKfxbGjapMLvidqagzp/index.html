Since the last review

Every automated test passed: 784 shapes, 133 templates, PowerPoint round trip, Figma import, slides and presenter view, the Claude chat, and every server route, including Figma and Canva sign-in and the plugin.

New

Figma and Canva

Sign in from Architect, a two-way Figma plugin, and most of both apps' everyday design tools.

New

Slides like PowerPoint

Transitions, builds, presenter view, laser pointer and editable .pptx in and out.

New

Claude studio

A chat that builds, finds and operates the app, with a picture upload and your animated character.

Fixed

Symbols and variables now save

They were kept in memory but left out of saved files.

Check

Needs your accounts to test

Figma and Canva sign-in need your own developer apps. Open a Visio export in Visio once.

What is still missing

  1. Route around shapes for connectors, and rich text inside one label.
  2. Email invites and resets, and database storage for very large organizations.
  3. Upload your own fonts, Word export and embedding in an intranet page.
  4. Canva's stock library, video and AI photos, and Figma's plugin ecosystem: these depend on outside services, so they stay out of an independent, offline-capable app.

Comparisons with Lucidchart, Visio, Figma and AutoCAD use their commonly known capabilities, not a fresh check of their current releases.
