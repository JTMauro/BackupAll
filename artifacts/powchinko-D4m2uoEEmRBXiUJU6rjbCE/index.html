How to play

Ladies and gentlemen

  • Aim the cannon with your mouse, or drag a finger. Click or let go to fire. Arrow keys fine-tune, Space fires.
  • Clear every orange peg to win the board. Lit pegs vanish when your ball drops out.
  • Blue 10 · Orange 100 · Purple 500. The bonus multiplier climbs to ×2, ×3, ×5 and ×10 as oranges fall.
  • Red star bumpers kick the ball back into play and are never used up.
  • Trick shots pay extra: Bank Shot (off a wall into an orange), Long Shot, Bumper Bonanza (3 bumpers) and Orange Rush (4 oranges in one shot).
  • Pop a floating ? balloon for a surprise: a free ball, bonus points or the board's power.
  • Spin the prize wheel after every board you clear to win points, extra balls or a Super Guide.
  • Land in the moving drum, or score 3,000 in one shot, for a free ball.
  • No game over: if you run out of balls, keep your cleared pegs and take more. Your board saves after every shot, so you can quit and resume any time.
  • Green pegs trigger the board's power: Multiball, Super Guide, Pow Blast, Fireball or Ghost Ball.
  • Hit the last orange to start Pow Fever. The ball drops into podiums worth up to 100,000, and every unused ball adds 10,000.
  • Level 101 is level 1 again with a twist: a few more oranges, a ball or two fewer, and everything moves.
