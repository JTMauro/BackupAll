Aim by dragging (or ← →), release or press Space to fire. One shot is in the air at a time. Three or more of a colour pop, and anything left hanging falls. Every few misses the asteroid front pushes down; if it crosses the red line, you're overrun. Swap with X.

Store

You earn 10 credits for every asteroid you shatter, 25 for every one that drifts loose and 250 for clearing a level. Gear stays in your inventory until you use it; tap its button on the right side during a level (or press its key).
