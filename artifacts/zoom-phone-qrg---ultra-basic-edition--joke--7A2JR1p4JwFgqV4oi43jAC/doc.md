# Zoom Phone QRG – Ultra Basic Edition (Joke)

2026-11-17 · @JT Mauro

Three steps. You've got this.

## 1. Phone rings

That noise is the phone. Not the microwave, not your coworker's ringtone, not a bird. If the screen is lit and the noise won't stop, it's the phone.

## 2. Pick up the phone

- Find the handset: the long, curved piece you'd feed a banana to.
- Lift it. Hold it with one hand. Two hands is allowed but unnecessary.
- Pressing the green button also works. It's green because it means go.

## 3. Speak into it

- The end with the holes goes near your mouth. The other end goes near your ear.
- Say "Hello." Words are optional but encouraged.
- Wait for the other person to say something back. This is called a conversation.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Phone doesn't ring | Stare at it. Intensely. |
| Nobody answers when you say hello | Say it again, louder. Slowly. |
| You can hear them but they can't hear you | Check that you're not talking into your elbow. |
| Call ended by itself | They hung up. It's not you. Probably. |
| Phone is on fire | Stop reading this guide. Call 911. |

## When you're done

Put the handset back. Do not throw it. Do not slam it. Gently, like it owes you nothing.

Still stuck? Contact IT. We will ask if you've restarted it.
