Six TVs, ranked

Four from the Costco floor plus two Vizio links. Scored on picture, gaming/PC hookup, value, size, owner and reviewer sentiment, and warranty. Prices are what was on the shelf tag or the last price I could verify.

Best overall: LG B6E 77" OLED. Nothing else here is in the same league for picture or PC/console use, and the Costco tag adds a 5-year plan.

Best value: Hisense U65 Pro 75". Real mini-LED, native 144Hz, 5-year coverage, under half the LG’s price.

Skip: the Vizio V755-J04 (2021-era, 60Hz, no local dimming, third-party Amazon seller).

Notes

  • Gaming weighted at 20% because a PC or console on HDMI 2.1 with VRR is where the 120/144Hz sets pull away from the 60Hz ones. If it’s strictly a movie/TV set, the Samsung moves closer to the Hisense.
  • If the Vizio Quantum Pro turns up at clearance pricing (~$400), it jumps to #2 on value; at ~$900+ it’s not worth it over the Hisense.
  • LG specs are from the B6/B6E reviews; Costco’s 77" B6E should perform the same. Hisense and LG NU87 are Costco-specific model numbers, so specs are taken from their retail equivalents.
  • Star ratings are from different retailers and aren’t directly comparable.
