## Laptop and Personal Belongings Security Reminder

2026-10-30 · @JT Mauro

**To:** All Cydcor Employees **From:** JT Mauro, IT **Subject:** Protect your laptop and personal belongings this holiday season

As we head into the holiday season, car break-ins tend to rise, especially in busy shopping centers and public lots. A quick reminder to help keep your company laptop and personal belongings safe.

**Best practice: take your laptop with you.** This is especially important when parking in public areas.

If you must leave your laptop or valuables in your car:

- Put them out of sight, ideally in the trunk.
- Move them to the trunk before you arrive, not in the parking lot where others can see.
- Lock your car, every time.
- Never leave bags, chargers, or cables visible. Even an empty bag can invite a break-in.

**If your laptop is lost or stolen,** contact IT immediately so we can lock the device and protect company data. Then file a police report.

Thank you for helping keep yourself and Cydcor secure. Have a safe and happy holiday season.
