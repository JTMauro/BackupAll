# Zoom Phone QRG – End User

2026-11-17 · @JT Mauro

## Getting started

Zoom Phone works in the Zoom app (desktop or mobile) and on your desk phone, all on one number. Open Zoom, sign in with your Cydcor account, click the **Phone** tab. Your number and extension: Phone → Settings. No Phone tab? Contact IT.

## Calls

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Call | Phone → Keypad → full number with area code → **Call**. Coworkers: dial the extension or type a name. |
| Answer / decline | **Accept** or **Decline** on the alert. Decline sends the caller to voicemail. |
| Mute / hold | **Mute** or **Hold** buttons |
| Transfer | **Transfer** → name or number → **Warm** (talk first) or **Blind** (send now) |
| Add a caller | **Add** → second person → **Merge** |
| Switch devices | **Flip** moves a live call between desktop, mobile and desk phone |
| Record | **Record** button, if IT enabled it |
| Need quiet | Set Zoom status to **Do Not Disturb**. Calls go to voicemail. |

## Voicemail and settings

- **Voicemail:** Phone → **Voicemail**. Record your greeting in Phone → Settings.
- **Forwarding, ring order, business hours:** zoom.us → Phone → Settings → Call Handling.
- Options greyed out are locked by IT.

## 911

Dial **911** from any Zoom Phone device and say your location out loud. Dispatchers see your registered emergency address (normally your office). Working remotely? Update your location when Zoom prompts you, or ask IT. Keep the line free in case they call back.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| No audio, one-way or choppy audio | Settings → Audio → test mic and speaker. Check headset is selected and unmuted. Move closer to Wi-Fi. |
| Doesn't ring on one device | Sign out of Zoom and back in. Check Do Not Disturb is off. |
| Desk phone dead or blank | Unplug, wait 10 seconds, plug back in. |

Still stuck? Contact IT Support: [add helpdesk email / extension]. Menu names can shift between Zoom releases.
