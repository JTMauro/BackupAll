Cydoodle

Our internal live quiz game. Creators build games in the Studio, the host puts them on the big screen, and everyone plays from their phone.

    1 · Build

    Creators sign in at /admin, write questions on the live design canvas, pick a theme, and can ask Claude for help using their own Claude account.

    2 · Host

    Publish, then press Host live. The big screen shows a 6-digit PIN and a QR code.

    3 · Play

    Players open the join link or scan the code, enter a nickname, and answer on their phone. Faster correct answers score more.
