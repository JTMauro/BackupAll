Briefing

Field manual

The cores

Raiders walk to your core vault, grab power cores and haul them back to an exit. Destroy a carrier and its cores drop, then float home on their own. Raiders passing a dropped core will pick it up. Lose every core and the sector falls.

Building

  • Raised metal pads hold towers beside the route.
  • Open grid floor (teal lines) can hold towers too, and raiders reroute around them. You can't seal the route completely.
  • Selling refunds everything before the first wave, 75% after.

Routes and fences

Pick Build routes in sector select to turn every walkway and pad into open floor. Electric fences cost 10 credits, block a tile, link up with neighboring fences and shock raiders walking past. Line them up to make long corridors past your towers. The glowing dots show the route raiders will take.

Support towers

A Command Post boosts damage of towers in range. A Reactor earns credits during waves. Cryo makes its target slower and more fragile. Shockwave stuns everything around it.

Ground and air

Gun, Laser, Cryo and Railgun hit both. Missile and Flak hit only flyers. Inferno, Cannon, Tesla, Temporal, Shockwave, Meteor and fences work on the ground. Armor blunts Gun, Cannon, Missile and Meteor hits; Laser, Tesla and Inferno ignore it. Tesla bursts tear through shields.

Controls

  • 1–9, 0 pick a build option, click to place, Esc or right-click to cancel
  • Drag or W A S D to pan, wheel to zoom
  • 360° view: right-drag (or middle-drag, Ctrl+drag) to spin and tilt, Q/E rotate, Page Up/Page Down tilt, O auto-orbit, Home reset. The camera pad on the right does the same.
  • U upgrade, Del sell, T cycle targeting
  • Space next wave, F game speed, P pause
  • Touch: tap a tile to preview, tap again to build. Pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to rotate.

Saving

Each of the three save slots keeps unlocked sectors, stars and one battle in progress. Save from the pause menu at any moment, or let autosave record each wave. Export a slot as a text code to move it to another browser.

Stars

Three stars for holding every core, two for keeping at least half, one for surviving. Elite difficulty scores 50% more.
