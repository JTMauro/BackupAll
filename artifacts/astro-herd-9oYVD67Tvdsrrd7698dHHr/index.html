    HOW TO PLAY

    Herding basics

    1. Aliens walk on their own. They turn around at walls and walk off ledges. You never steer them directly.
    2. Pick gear, then click the ground. Choose a button in the gear bar (or press 1–0, B), then click a spot the aliens will walk over. A menu asks which direction (left, right, up, down) or how many aliens, then places a beacon. The next alien to cross it uses the gear exactly that way.
    3. Beacons. Jetpack beacons never run out. Click a beacon again to add a charge, or to flip the arrow on Grapple, Laser and Cutter. Right-click removes it.
    4. Barrier. A force-field wall. Click it to open or close. Use two to pen the herd somewhere safe.
    5. Prefer clicking aliens? Switch CLICK ON to ALIENS (or press M).
    6. Match colors. Each color only enters its own colored portal.
    7. Speed. 1× is slow on purpose. Use the SPEED buttons to go up to 10× when you want to finish. Pause with Space. You can place gear while paused.
    8. Camera. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom, right-drag or the cube button for 3D.

    New here? Play Training Ground. It stops at every station and shows you what to click.
