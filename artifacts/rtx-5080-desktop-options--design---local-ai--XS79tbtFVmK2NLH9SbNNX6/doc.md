#### Expansion options

- **RAM:** Amazon's spec lists DDR5 with a 256GB maximum, and the 32GB listing shows 2 or more open slots ([Amazon spec](https://www.amazon.com/Panorama-i9-14900KF-PC-Warranty-Internal/dp/B0DXFVSDNX)). The 64GB version is sold directly, which avoids buying RAM separately.
- **Storage:** the listings state only the installed 2TB drive. Spare M.2 or SATA slots aren't stated, so ask the seller before counting on a second drive.
- **GPU:** the RTX 5080's 16GB is fixed. The listings don't give case clearance or power supply wattage, so confirm both before planning a 24GB card later.

The 5080's 16GB of VRAM is plenty for design work and for AI models up to roughly 14B parameters. For larger models, a 24GB card (used 4090 or 3090) is the better path. The rest of this doc covers alternatives to the Panorama.

For 64GB or more, 2x32GB or 2x48GB is cleaner than filling four slots, since four DDR5 sticks often run slower. RAM is expensive right now: one compatibility seller lists Kingston DDR5-5600 at $189 for 32GB and $429 for 48GB, and [Windows Central](https://windowscentral.com/hardware/desktops/alienware-aurora-gaming-desktop-act1250-feb-2026) describes a continuing RAM crunch. Buying a 32GB base and upgrading later only if needed is the cheaper route.

GPU swaps: the listings I saw do not state case clearance or power headroom beyond the PSU wattage (850W on the Skytech and STORMCRAFT units), so ask the seller before planning for a larger 24GB card.

### Before you buy

Wait for Black Friday (Nov 27) if you can: one [price guide](https://www.shopback.com/blog/electronics/nvidia-rtx-5090-supply-shortage-2026-us-what-to-buy-instead) calls it the real GPU price trough at Best Buy and Newegg.

- Confirm the exact CPU in the listing title, since similar names sometimes cover different processors.
- Check the seller (brand or Amazon) and the return window.
- Windows 11 Pro comes with the Panorama and MSI units; the Skytech and CyberPower listings say Home or just "Windows 11".
- The 5080 has 16GB of VRAM. A 24GB RTX 5080 is rumored for Q1 2027 ([report](https://www.noobfeed.com/hardware/nvidia-rtx-5080-24gb-16gb-model)), but another outlet says it was [cancelled](https://en.gamegpu.com/news/zhelezo/nvidia-otmenila-geforce-rtx-5080-super-s-24-gb-eshche-do-nachala-proizvodstva). Don't buy on the rumor.
- For AI-heavy work, a used RTX 4090 or 3090 (24GB) is usually better value than a 5080.
