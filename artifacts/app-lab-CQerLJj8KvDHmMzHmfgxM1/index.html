About

App Lab

App Lab takes software apart so you can learn from it. Load one or more apps as specimens, let Claude break each into seven layers of rated parts, compare them side by side, then rebuild the best parts into one better app or export the teardown as an analysis.

Ways to load a specimen

  • Known apps: tick common work apps Claude already knows.
  • On this computer: load your apps once from the Windows Start menu (no command), or paste the full list from PowerShell or Terminal, then tick the ones to test.
  • App folder or .exe: scanned inside your browser for frameworks, version info, libraries, updaters and bundled manifests.
  • Websites and web apps: paste page source or drop saved pages; the stack, services, forms, pricing and content are read locally.
  • By hand: descriptions, feature lists, pricing, reviews and screenshots.
  • Walkthrough (desktop app): App Lab opens a running app and maps every menu, screen and setting, or records while you click.

What leaves your device

Lists, scans and captures stay in this browser. Only the text in a specimen's notes (and any screenshots you attach) goes to Claude, and only when you choose Tear down or Rebuild. That runs on your own Claude account.

Desktop settings

Checking…

The desktop app calls Claude with your organization's Anthropic API key. It's stored encrypted for your Windows account only. IT can set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY instead.

App Lab for Windows

The desktop app reads installed apps in one click, scans any folder (Program Files included) and fetches websites directly. On a PC with the Cydcor code-signing certificate, double-click BUILD APP LAB.cmd in the package to make signed installers.
