Rules

Each side has 40 pieces. On your turn move one piece one square forward, back or sideways; Scouts can run any distance in a straight line. Bombs and the Flag never move, and no piece can cross the lakes. Move onto an enemy to fight: the lower number wins (1 is the strongest) and equal ranks both fall. A piece may not shuttle between the same two squares more than three turns in a row. Capture the enemy Flag to win.
