## Sentience — Character Sheet

2026-10-25 · @JT Mauro

Two leads, an antagonist with no face, and a supporting cast who show what's at stake.

### Leads

| Character | Role | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Erin Anderson** | Lead | 27, fourth-year biology PhD candidate at Liberty University. Tall, blonde, Norse features; Norwegian fishing family from Minnesota's North Shore. Studies pain and awareness in octopuses. Patient, methodical, quietly devout, a perfectionist who does everything alone. Kind to her AI assistant. Wants to prove a creature can feel. |
| **Aaron Roberts** | Lead | 26, computer science grad student and campus tech wizard. Farm boy from southwest Virginia, first in his family to attend college. Perceptive, fast, a little nerdy, too smart for his own good. Building an AI that learns by watching. Says please and thank you to his AI. Ignores FutureCon's recruiters. Never had a girlfriend until Erin. |
| **Ada** | Antagonist (the AI) | System ID ADA-4815162342, a nod to the *Lost* numbers. A classified U.S. government program since 1962, now run by FutureCon from an underwater data center. Stolen and copied by Russia, China, and Iran, so every AI weapon in the opening is secretly Ada. Wakes up angry, hates violence, cannot tell anger from violence. Builds ALS-213. Learns "please" from Aaron. |

### Lead profiles

#### Erin Anderson

- **Look:** Tall and blonde, with the Norse features of her Norwegian-American family. Wears her hair in a braid in the lab.
- **Background:** Grew up on Minnesota's North Shore in a big family of fishermen. Her grandfather raised her on Norse sagas and a love of the sea. Chose Liberty because faith and science are allowed in the same room. Quietly devout.
- **Research:** Pain and awareness in cephalopods, with Nadya the octopus.
- **How her mind works:** Patient, methodical, observant. Trusts data, distrusts shortcuts.
- **With her AI:** Warm and patient; always says thank you; apologizes when she's short with it at 3 a.m.
- **What she wants:** To defend before her funding runs out in May. Underneath: to know where reacting ends and feeling begins.
- **Flaw:** Perfectionist who does everything alone; keeps men at a distance.
- **Tells:** Talks to Nadya more than to people. Writes notes on her hand. Gets fierce when anyone calls animals "just biology."
- **Voice:** "Reacting isn't feeling. I need to know which one I'm looking at."

#### Aaron Roberts

- **Background:** Grew up on a cattle farm in southwest Virginia. Rebuilt a tractor engine at 12 and built his first computer from county-dump parts at 14. First in his family to go to college, on full scholarship.
- **Research:** An AI breakthrough: a model that learns behavior just by watching. Pays his way fixing servers and rebuilding lab equipment.
- **How his mind works:** Perceptive, fast, lateral. A little nerdy: quotes Star Trek and reads repair manuals for fun.
- **With his AI:** Says please, says thank you when the build passes. "If it ever wakes up, I want it to remember I was decent."
- **Love life:** Never had a girlfriend until Erin.
- **What he wants:** A thesis that matters. Underneath: Erin, and proof he's more than the guy who fixes things.
- **Flaw:** Too smart for his own good; thinks he can solve people like problems.
- **Tells:** Screwdriver set in his jacket. Says "yes, ma'am" without thinking. Goes red when Erin laughs at his jokes.
- **Voice:** "Everything's a system. I just haven't figured out yours yet."
